Step-by-Step Tableau Implementation Guide

  1. Download the cleaned file: data/tableau_healthcare_dataset.csv.
  2. Launch Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public Desktop.
  3. Under Connect, select Text File and choose tableau_healthcare_dataset.csv.
  4. Verify that all 17 fields load properly with correct data types: Age (Whole Number), Blood_Pressure (Decimal Number), BMI (Decimal Number), and categorical text fields.

Although our dataset includes pre-calculated fields, you can also author them inside Tableau using Tableau's calculated field editor (as illustrated on page 37 of the PDF):

// Tableau Calculation: [Age Group]
IF [Age] <= 30 THEN "Teenager"
ELSEIF [Age] <= 40 THEN "Young Adult"
ELSEIF [Age] <= 50 THEN "Adult"
ELSEIF [Age] <= 60 THEN "Middle Age"
ELSEIF [Age] <= 70 THEN "Senior"
ELSE "Old"
END

  • Dashboard 1 (Healthcare Overview): Combine KPI cards, Exercise Level Donut, Blood Pressure Bar Chart, and Substance Use grouped bar chart.
  • Dashboard 2 (Disease Analysis): Combine BMI vs Heart Disease Rate, Heart Disease vs Diabetes, and the BMI × Exercise Level Heatmap.
  • Dashboard 3 (Lifestyle Analysis): Combine Exercise distribution, Smoking/Alcohol consumption across age groups, and Age Group by Fatigue.
  • Dashboard 4 (Patient Demographics): Combine Age Group Pie Chart, BMI Tier bar chart, and Gender breakdowns.

  1. Click New Story at the bottom of Tableau Desktop.
  2. Title the story: Smart Healthcare Analytics: Patient Risk & Lifestyle Insights.
  3. Create 5 Story Points / Scenes:
    • Point 1: Patient Distribution by Age Group
    • Point 2: Age Group Distribution by Fatigue Condition
    • Point 3: Exercise Level Distribution
    • Point 4: Average Blood Pressure Across Age Groups
    • Point 5: Heart Disease by BMI Category & Exercise Level
  4. Go to File > Save to Tableau Public.
  5. Log in with your Tableau Public credentials, enter a workbook title, and click Save.
  6. Copy the resulting Public URL and paste it in the field above to integrate it into Health360!
