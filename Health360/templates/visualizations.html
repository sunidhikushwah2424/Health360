Chart #2 Bar Chart – Average Blood Pressure Across Age Groups

Vascular Profile by Life Stage
Clinical Interpretation

Average systolic blood pressure hovers between 133.61 mmHg (Adults) and 135.67 mmHg (Old age group), demonstrating that elevated arterial tension is prevalent across both young and senior demographics.

Reference Threshold: 130 mmHg marks the clinical boundary for Stage 1 Hypertension. The entire cohort's average sits above this line.

Chart #3 Grouped Bar Chart – Alcohol and Smoking Consumption Across Age Groups

Substance Consumption Patterns
Behavioral Observations

Alcohol consumption consistently outpaces tobacco smoking in every age bracket. Young Adults and Teenagers exhibit notable rates of social alcohol intake (~38%), while tobacco use maintains steady prevalence across Middle Age and Senior cohorts.

Chart #4 Donut Chart – Exercise Level Distribution

Physical Exertion Demographics
Exertion Profile

The dataset exhibits a virtually equal tripartite split: Sedentary (32.94%), Moderate (33.54%), and Active (33.52%). This balanced baseline enables unbiased comparison of physical activity's protective effects.

Chart #6 Grouped Bar Chart – Heart Disease vs Diabetes Prevalence by BMI Category

Chronic Condition Comparison
Comparative Morbidity

Both heart disease and diabetes escalate in lockstep as body mass index transitions from normal to overweight and obese, highlighting the mutual metabolic roots of cardiac and glycemic dysregulation.

Chart #7 Bar Chart – BMI Category vs Heart Disease Rate

Cardiovascular Risk Escalation
Risk Gradient

Patients diagnosed with obesity manifest an average heart disease rate of approximately 39.98%, compared to 32.91% for overweight, 25.97% for normal weight, and 20.64% for underweight patients.

Chart #8 Heat Map – Heart Disease by BMI Category and Exercise Level

Bivariate Risk Matrix
Matrix Insights

The highest risk cell in the entire matrix corresponds to Obese + Sedentary patients (42.86% prevalence), whereas active habits mitigate cardiovascular risk across every single BMI tier.

Chart #9 Bar Chart – Age Group Distribution by Fatigue Condition

Symptomatic Burden Across Life Stages
Symptomatic Distribution

Fatigue condition displays a remarkably consistent distribution across age tiers, averaging ~400-440 reported cases per age group, underscoring fatigue as a ubiquitous clinical complaint spanning all adult life stages.
